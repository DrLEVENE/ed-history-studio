Patient

How the history was obtained

Presenting complaints

Write each complaint as it should read after “presents with”.

Observations

Photo or screenshot of the triage observations. It is read on this device and never uploaded or saved. Check every value before filling. The first read downloads about 7 MB.

History

Tap what applies. Anything left untouched stays out of the note.

Associated symptoms

+ = present. − = explicitly denied, recorded under Patient denies (or attributed to the history source). Symptoms left unselected are omitted from the note. Review every symptom before copying.

Examination

Tap what you found. Blanks stay out of the note, and nothing is recorded as normal unless you select it. Record consent and chaperone for intimate examinations.

Investigations

Read blood results from a photo, a saved image, a pasted screenshot or pasted text. Images are read on this device and never uploaded or saved. Check every result before adding it.

Scores

Published scoring systems. Answers you give elsewhere on the page (observations, age, symptoms, history and exam findings) are filled in for you to check. A score goes into the note only once every item is answered.

Diagnosis support

The three most likely diagnoses for this presentation, ranked by the findings you have recorded, with must-not-miss diagnoses. Worked out on this device from a fixed list of rules; nothing is sent anywhere. It is a prompt, not a diagnosis. Only diagnoses you tick go into the note.

Plan

Harriet Lane automatic formulary: pending verified edition and medicine list. The calculator below only multiplies a clinician-entered prescription; it does not select or validate a dose. Use mg/kg/dose, not mg/kg/day. Neonatal regimens and age, indication, route, renal and hepatic adjustments require separate verification.

Clinical note
Builds itself as you enter findings. Edit it here before copying into Symone. This tool documents your entries. Scores show the published bands only; nothing here recommends treatment.
Copy section
No direct connection to Symone. Copy and paste into the appropriate note field.
